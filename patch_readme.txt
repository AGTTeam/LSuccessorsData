 == SPRITE RECOLORS ==
This patch is available in two versions. In the "sprite-recolors" patch, the character sprites for Matt, Hatori, and Jack have been recolored to better match their anime counterparts. In contrast, the "no-recolors" patch features the characters sprites as they were in the original Japanese release.
